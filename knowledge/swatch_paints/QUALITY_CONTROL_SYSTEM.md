# 🟡 3. STAGE 1: RAW MATERIAL QC (INPUT DEFENSE)

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
Substandard input raw material ko factory entry gate par hi block karna.

---

## ⚙️ RAW MATERIAL TESTING PROTOCOL:

### 🧩 1. SILICA / FILLER POWDER:
- **Mesh Consistency Test:** Sieve through 80-100 mesh sieve. Ensure 100% passage with zero coarse grit or oversized particles.
- **Moisture Content Check:** Oven dry sample test. Moisture MUST be $< 0.5\%$. Reject if powder is clumpy or damp.

### 🧩 2. ACRYLIC BINDER & RESIN:
- **Solid Content Test:** Measure non-volatile matter percentage ($50\% \pm 1\%$ standard).
- **Viscosity & Odor Test:** Check for bacterial spoilage or foul odor. Reject if sour smelling or watery.

### 🧩 3. WATER PURITY:
- **TDS & pH Test:** Total Dissolved Solids MUST be $< 300 \text{ ppm}$, pH 7.0 neutral. Reject untreated hard water.

---

## ❌ MANDATORY RAW MATERIAL REJECTION RULES:
- Reject any binder lot showing signs of phase separation or bacterial odor.
- Reject filler bags stored in damp conditions showing moisture clumping.

---

## 🎯 GOLDEN RULE:
> **Raw material me compromise = Pure 500 kg batch ka direct financial loss!**

---

# 🟢 4. STAGE 2: IN-PROCESS QC (DURING MANUFACTURING)

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
Manufacturing mistakes ko mixing tank ke andar hi identify aur fix karna.

---

## ⚙️ IN-PROCESS CHECKPOINTS:

### ✔ 1. PRE-MIX HOMOGENEITY:
- Verify complete dissolution of thickeners and biocides before powder charging.
- Check for foam buildup (Add defoamer if foam level exceeds 5% vessel volume).

### ✔ 2. DISPERSION & PARTICLE BREAKDOWN:
- Dip gauge check to ensure zero undispersed dry lumps.

### ✔ 3. TEMPERATURE MONITORING:
- Digital infrared thermometer check every 10 minutes.
- **Critical Limit:** If batch temp reaches $40^\circ\text{C}$, trigger water cooling jacket and pause disperser for 5 minutes!

---

## ❌ IN-PROCESS REJECTION TRIGGERS:
- Excessive micro-foaming that fails to settle after deaeration.
- Batch temperature exceeding $45^\circ\text{C}$ (causes polymer gelation).

---

# 🔵 5. STAGE 3: FINAL PRODUCT QC TESTS

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
Finished coating batch ki commercial suitability certify karna.

---

## 🧪 TEST 1: VISUAL & PHYSICAL CHECK
- **Smoothness:** Zero grit or un-wetted filler lumps.
- **Color Matching:** Compare batch sample against Master Shade Card under D65 standard daylight lamp.

---

## 🧪 TEST 2: WALL APPLICATION & SPREAD TEST
- Apply batch sample on a $1 \text{ m} \times 1 \text{ m}$ cement board using standard applicator trowel.
- **Check:** Smooth trowel glide, zero sagging, crisp texture pattern definition.

---

## 🧪 TEST 3: DRYING TIME & CRACK RESISTANCE
- **Surface Dry Time:** Must dry within **45–60 minutes** at ambient $30^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Mud Cracking Test:** Apply thick $3 \text{ mm}$ patch. Ensure zero shrinkage or hairline cracks upon complete curing.

---

## 🧪 TEST 4: VISCOSITY & WET DENSITY
- Measure viscosity using Stormer Viscometer: **$110 - 120 \text{ KU}$** target.
- Density Check: $1.65 - 1.75 \text{ g/cm}^3$ for Swatch Rustic Texture.

---

## ❌ MANDATORY BATCH REJECTION CONDITIONS:
- Hairline cracking or mud-cracking on sample wall panel.
- Viscosity $< 105 \text{ KU}$ (too runny) or $> 125 \text{ KU}$ (too stiff/unusable).
- Color shade deviation $> \Delta E 0.5$ from master standard.

# 🔴 7. REJECTION MANAGEMENT & RECOVERY SOP

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
Substandard batch scrap financial loss ko minimize karna.

---

## ⚙️ DEFECT CLASSIFICATION & RECOVERY PROTOCOL:

### 🧩 MINOR DEFECTS (RECOVERABLE):
- *Symptom:* Viscosity slightly lower than spec ($100 \text{ KU}$).
- *Action:* Add pre-dissolved HEC thickener under low-speed agitation to bring to $115 \text{ KU}$.

### 🧩 MAJOR DEFECTS (NON-RECOVERABLE / SCRAP):
- *Symptom:* Mud cracking due to low binder ratio, or severe bacterial contamination.
- *Action:* Full batch dump into scrap pit. Log incident report in Hermes via `/sop`.

---

## 🎯 REJECTION MANDATE:
> **Never patch up a major defect to save money. Market reputation is priceless!**
