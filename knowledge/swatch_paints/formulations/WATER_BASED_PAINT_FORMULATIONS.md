## Interior Emulsion Paint (Premium Grade)

| Component | Function | Percentage (%) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acrylic Emulsion (Binder) | Film formation, adhesion | 25–30% | Pure acrylic, 45–50% solids; Tronox or Cristal high-performance grade recommended |
| Titanium Dioxide (TiO₂) | Opacity, whiteness | 16–18% | Rutile R-grade; optimized for high coverage and contrast ratio |
| Calcium Carbonate (Filler) | Extender, bulk | 20–25% | Co-hiding extender; paired with TiO₂ for cost efficiency |
| HEC Thickener | Viscosity control | 0.5–0.8% | Hydroxyethyl cellulose; provides in-can viscosity and open time |
| Dispersant | Pigment wetting | 0.4–0.6% | Anionic polymeric dispersant; ensures fine pigment dispersion (Hegman ≥5) |
| Biocide (In-can) | Preservation | 0.1–0.3% | MIT/CMIT blend; prevents microbial growth and ensures 18-month shelf life |
| Defoamer | Foam control | 0.1–0.3% | Silicone-based; low-foam wetting agents reduce foam at source |
| Coalescing Agent | Film formation aid | 1.5–2.0% | Propylene glycol; aids coalescence in cooler climates |
| Water | Carrier | 30–35% | Final dilution; adjusts viscosity for application |

**Target Properties:**
- KU Viscosity: 95–115
- Hegman Grind: ≥5
- Scrub Resistance: 1000+ cycles
- Drying Time: 1–2 hours (touch-dry)

## General Guidelines

- **Grind Paste:** Use high-speed disperser (HSD) for 15–20 minutes, followed by bead mill for Hegman ≥5.
- **Let-Down:** Add binder, coalescent, and final additives slowly under medium agitation.
- **Filtration:** Pass through 100–150 mesh filter to remove coarse particles.
- **Quality Checks:** Viscosity, pH, hiding power, drying time, and scrub resistance.
- **Storage:** Store in sealed containers at 5–35°C; shelf life: 12–18 months.
