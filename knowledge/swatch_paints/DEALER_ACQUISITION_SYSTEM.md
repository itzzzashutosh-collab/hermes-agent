# 🎯 CORE PHILOSOPHY & ACQUISITION RULE
> **Target → Enter → Qualify → Close → Activate**

Swatch Paints does not compete for shelf space by begging or offering endless credit.  
We acquire dealers by demonstrating **Counter Profit, Zero-Machine Simplicity, and Fast Rotation.**

---

# ⚙️ 5-STEP DEALER ACQUISITION ARCHITECTURE

---

## 🎯 STEP 1: TARGET IDENTIFICATION & PROFILING

### A. High-Potential Dealer Criteria
- **High Footfall Location:** Main paint market, hardware cluster, or building material hub.
- **Active Painter Network:** Minimum 15-20 regular painters/applicators visiting the shop weekly.
- **Existing Coating Sales:** Selling interior/exterior emulsions, wall putty, textures, or waterproofing chemicals.
- **Territory Clearance:** No existing exclusive Swatch Paints DGP dealer within a 3-5 km radius.

### B. Pre-Visit Intelligence Gathering (Field Rep SOP)
1. Observe the shop counter for 5 minutes (Which brands are moving? Is it Asian, Berger, or local brands?).
2. Identify the owner/decision-maker (Don't pitch to counter boys).
3. Check shop branding (Is the dealer open to co-branding?).

---

## 🎯 STEP 2: FIELD ENTRY & INITIAL APPROACH SOP

### A. The 60-Second Counter Entry Hook
> **Sales Rep:** *"Namaste [Dealer Name] Sir! Main Sharma Industries / Swatch Paints se hoon."*  
> **Sales Rep:** *"Sir, ek simple 30-second sawal — aapke counter par 20L emulsion ya 25kg texture bag par kitna net margin nikalta hai?"*  
> *(Pause 3 seconds → let dealer answer)*  
> **Sales Rep:** *"Agar mai aapko same premium finish category me 2x se 3x per-unit margin dikha du, toh kya aap 5 minute consider karoge?"*

### B. Sample Board Presentation (Visual Impact)
- Open the **Swatch Rustic Texture & Roller Coat Sample Folder** immediately on the counter.
- Let the dealer touch and feel the scratch-resistant, weather-durable finish.
- Highlight: *"No tinting machine needed, zero colorant inventory stock lock."*

---

## 🎯 STEP 3: QUALIFYING THE DEALER (NEPQ & SPIN FRAMEWORK)

Before presenting the closing kit, the Sales Rep MUST qualify the dealer across 4 parameters:

### A. Volume Capacity
- *"Sir, aapka monthly texture aur premium emulsion ka average volume kitna bag/bucket nikalta hai?"*

### B. Profit Expectation
- *"Aapka current margin 3% se 5% ke beech hai. Kya aap saal me ₹2,000,000+ extra counter profit target karna chahte hain?"*

### C. Painter Ecosystem
- *"Aapke dukan par top 5 contractors ya painters kaun se hain jo major sites chalate hain?"*

### D. Decision Maker & Financial Discipline
- *"Order and payment authorization aap khud handle karte hain ya partner?"*

---

## 🎯 STEP 4: CONVERSION & SEGP 20-BAG TRIAL CLOSING

### A. Swatch Trial Growth Program (SEGP) Presentation
- Do not pitch 100 bags on day 1.
- Offer the **SEGP 20-Bag Starter Pack** (Swatch Rustic Texture / Core Fast-Moving Emulsions).

### B. The Gap Build Closing Pitch
> **Sales Rep:** *"Sir, 50 bags legacy brand ke bechne par aapko max ₹3,500 milta hai. Swatch Paints ke 50 bags par aapka direct net margin ₹20,000+ banta hai."*  
> **Sales Rep:** *"Lambi baat nahi karte. Trial ke liye 20-bag SEGP starter kit se shuru karte hain. Risk low hai, margin clear hai. 20-bag ka order bill karau?"*

### C. Risk Mitigation Guarantee
- *"Sir, 20 bag 15 din me rotate honge. Isme humari field team aakar aapke local painters ko activate karegi. Stock dead rehne ka sawal hi nahi."*

---

## 🎯 STEP 5: COUNTER ACTIVATION & STOCK ROTATION SOP

Acquiring the order is only 20% of the job; 80% is rotating the stock from the dealer's shop.

### Day 1: Stock Delivery & Placement
- Ensure prominent front-display stack of Swatch buckets/bags at the counter.
- Paste Swatch Saathi QR Code posters on the dealer's main counter.

### Day 2: Painter Network Integration (SPGP Setup)
- Collect contact details of 15 painters connected to the dealer.
- Register all 15 painters into **Swatch Saathi WhatsApp System** (`/balance`, `/rewards`).
- Credit first 10 welcome tokens to painters under that dealer's referral tag.

### Day 3-5: Live Wall Demonstration & Site Push
- Conduct a 30-minute live texture wall application demo at the dealer's shop or nearby site.
- Invite top 5 contractors to see the application speed and coverage.

### Day 7 & Day 14: Replenishment Review
- Track stock balance via Hermes `/stock_status`.
- When stock drops to 5 bags, trigger automatic replenishment order for next tier (DGP Upgrade).
