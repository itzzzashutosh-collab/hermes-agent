# ⚙️ 2. PER-UNIT ECONOMICS FORMULA

$$\text{Net Factory Profit Per Bag} = \text{Factory Selling Price} - \left( \text{Raw Material} + \text{Packaging} + \text{Labour} + \text{Overhead} + \text{Freight} \right)$$

# 🟢 4. SELLING PRICE TIER STRUCTURE

---

## 🎯 4-TIER PRICING WATERFALL:

```
[Factory Cost: ₹550] ──► [Factory Selling: ₹650] ──► [Dealer Purchase: ₹650] ──► [Retail MRP: ₹1,150]
```

### 1. FACTORY SELLING PRICE (EX-FACTORY / DEALER BUYING):
- **Price:** ₹650 / Bag
- **Factory Margin:** $₹650 - ₹550 = \mathbf{₹100 \text{ Net Profit / Bag}}$ (15.38% Factory Net Margin).

### 2. DEALER SELLING PRICE (TO APPLICATOR / CONTRACTOR):
- **Price:** ₹950 – ₹1,000 / Bag
- **Dealer Margin:** $₹950 - ₹650 = \mathbf{₹300 \text{ Net Counter Margin / Bag}}$ (31.5% Counter Margin!).

### 3. RETAIL CONSUMER MRP:
- **MRP:** ₹1,150 / Bag
- **Consumer Savings:** Consumer gets premium finish at ₹1,150 vs MNC legacy alternatives at ₹1,800+.

# 🔴 7. BREAK-EVEN & FIXED COST COVERAGE

---

## 🎯 BREAK-EVEN QUANTITY FORMULA:

$$\text{Monthly Break-Even Volume (Bags)} = \frac{\text{Total Monthly Fixed Overhead Costs}}{\text{Net Factory Profit Per Bag (₹100)}}$$

---

## 🧩 APPLIED BREAK-EVEN CASE STUDY:
- **Factory Rent & Utilities:** ₹35,000 / month
- **Salaries & Staff:** ₹30,000 / month
- **Total Monthly Fixed Costs:** ₹65,000 / month
- **Net Factory Contribution:** ₹100 / bag

$$\text{Break-Even Volume} = \frac{₹65,000}{₹100} = \mathbf{650 \text{ Bags / Month}}$$

> **Execution Metric:** Selling just **26 bags/day** completely covers all factory fixed overheads! Every bag sold after 650 is **100% pure net profit**!
