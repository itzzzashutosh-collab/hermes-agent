# Swatch Paints — Marketing Plan, Trade Schemes & Promotions

## 1. Marketing Strategy & Positioning
- **Core Value Proposition:** High Quality at Factory-Direct Pricing ("Shandar Quality, Sahi Daam").
- **Target Channels:** Retail Paint Dealers, Hardware Stores, Painting Contractors, Real-Estate Builders.

---

## 2. Active Trade Schemes & Dealer Offers
1. **Volume Growth Bonus (Quarterly):**
   - Tier 1 (₹ 5 Lakhs uplift): Additional 3% cash rebate / Gold Coin.
   - Tier 2 (₹ 10 Lakhs uplift): Additional 5% cash rebate / International Trip voucher.

2. **Festive Special Schemes (Diwali / Seasonal):**
   - Scratch Card per 20L Barrel purchase with instant Paytm/UPI cashback.
   - Gift hampers on minimum 50-bucket stock orders.

---

## 3. Contractor & Field Marketing Activities
- **Painter Meets:** Monthly local painter gatherings with product demonstration & lunch.
- **Sample Kit Distribution:** Mini sample tubs (200ml) distributed to architects and top contractors.
- **Signboard & Branding Subsidies:** 50-100% shop board branding reimbursement for exclusive Swatch Paints dealers.
