## 💰 3. Dealer Growth Programs (Primary Engine)

### 🟡 A. Swatch Trial Growth Program (STGP)
- **Objective:** New dealer onboarding without hesitation.
- **Structure:**
  - **Initial Entry:** 20 Bags Starter Program
  - **Payment:** Flexible onboarding model
  - **Shelf Risk:** Controlled via exchange support
- **Value Delivered:** Low-risk entry, immediate earning visibility, quick product testing.

### 🟢 B. Swatch Dealership Growth Program (DGP)
- **Objective:** Convert serious dealers into long-term exclusive partners.
- **Eligibility:** Minimum monthly movement commitment + Territory discipline agreement.
- **Benefits:**
  - Area-level protection (controlled distribution)
  - Higher slab-based margins
  - Priority stock allocation
  - Structured business growth roadmap

### 🔵 C. Volume Performance Incentive System
- **Objective:** Push secondary sales & repeat orders.
- **Structure:**
  - **Tier 1 (₹ 5 Lakh uplift):** 3% additional earning
  - **Tier 2 (₹ 10 Lakh uplift):** 5% additional earning
  - **Tier 3 (High volume partners):** Premium rewards / travel incentives
- **Rule:** All incentives tied to actual market movement, not just inventory loading.

### 🟣 D. Counter Profit Enhancement Model
- **Core Idea:** Dealer should feel: *“Ye product mere counter ka profit badhata hai.”*
- **Execution:**
  - High per-bag earning vs traditional brands
  - Faster rotation vs slow stock
  - No heavy machine dependency

- **Objective:** Painters = Demand creators.
- **Structure:** Per bag earning token system + Tier-based reward accumulation.
- **Reward Slabs:**
