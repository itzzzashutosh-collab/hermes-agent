## 🎯 2. PROBING ARCHITECTURE (SPIN + NEPQ METHODOLOGY)

### A. Situation Questions (Current Baseline)
- *"Sir, aapke counter par monthly kitne bags texture ya emulsions rotate hote hain?"*
- *"Aapka major volume MNC brands (Asian/Berger) me nikalta hai ya local regional brands me?"*
- *"Machine maintenance aur colorant inventory me kitna capital block rehta hai?"*

### B. Problem Questions (Uncovering Friction)
- *"Kya aapko lagta hai ki legacy brands me 3-5% margin par kaam karke shop overheads cover karna mushkil ho raha hai?"*
- *"Jab painter doosre brand ka demand karta hai, toh kya aapko per-bag margin compromise karna padta hai?"*

### C. Implication Questions (Building Urgency)
- *"Sir, agar har mahine 100 bags par aap ₹200 extra margin miss kar rahe ho, toh saal me ₹2,400,000 ka net profit counter se nikal raha hai. Kya ye sahi hai?"*

### D. Need-Payoff Questions (Creating Value)
- *"Agar Swatch Paints ke sath aapko per bag direct ₹240-₹300 net earning mile aur painter ko instant QR cashback, toh counter ka rotation kitna tez ho jayega?"*
