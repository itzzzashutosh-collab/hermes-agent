## 1. Corporate Vision & Executive Mission
Swatch Paints is an emerging leader in high-performance, eco-friendly decorative and protective coatings. Manufactured under Sharma Industries, Swatch Paints combines advanced resin technology with weather-resistant formulation tailored for Indian climate conditions.
