---

## 🟥 CASE 1: DEALER REFUSES PURCHASE ORDER

- **Root Cause Diagnosis:**  
  - *Price/Margin Concern:* Margin gap vs. legacy brands.  
  - *Demand Uncertainty:* Counter fear of unsold inventory.  
  - *Trust Deficit:* Unfamiliarity with Swatch Paints durability.  

- **Automated Decision Flow:**  
  1. **Step 1 — SPIN / NEPQ Probing:** Execute Socratic questions to surface the true bottleneck (`/sales_probe`).  
  2. **Step 2 — Chris Voss Engine:** Deploy Tactical Labeling & Accusation Audit (`"It sounds like you're worried about capital being locked up..."`).  
  3. **Step 3 — Alex Hormozi Offer Redesign:** Pivot to the zero-risk **SEGP Starter Pack** (20 bags @ ₹260/bag with 100% money-back quality guarantee).  

- **Final Decision Move:** Convert high-friction 100-bag refusal into a frictionless 20-bag trial order with immediate painter token linkage.

  1. Activate pre-qualified **Secondary Chemical Suppliers** (Direct factory sourcing).

## 🟥 CASE 2: BATCH QUALITY FAILURE ($<110 \text{ KU}$ Viscosity or Mud-Cracking)

- **Automated Decision Flow:**  
  1. **Immediate Quarantine:** Issue automated dispatch freeze on Batch ID in ERP.

  3. **Root Cause Analysis (RCA):** Audit raw material lot quality and disperser mixing temperature ($<35^\circ\text{C}$).
