# 🧲 2. STAGE 1: LEAD TARGETING PHASE

- **Primary Objective:** Identify high-potential paint counters using the Ideal Dealer Profile (IDP).  
- **Targeting Criteria:**  
  - Active retail presence in Putty, Primer, & Emulsions.  
  - Established counter footfall ($>10\text{ walk-ins/day}$).  
  - Existing local painter network ($>5\text{ active contractors}$).  
- **Execution SOP:**  
  1. Conduct micro-cluster market reconnaissance; shortlist 20 target dealers per territory.  
  2. Map daily TSO route plan (`/route_plan`).  
  3. Execute initial SPIN/NEPQ probing call (`/sales_probe`).

---

# ⚔️ 3. STAGE 2: TRIAL ONBOARDING PHASE (SEGP ENTRY)

- **Primary Objective:** Frictionless entry with zero risk exposure.  
- **Execution SOP:**  
  1. Pitch the **SEGP (Swatch Early Growth Partner)** starter pack (20 bags Rustic Putty @ ₹260/bag).  
  2. Provide 100% money-back quality guarantee & slow-moving stock exchange commitment.  
  3. Complete instant ERP registration (`/new_dealer`).  
- **Stage Success Metric:** 20-bag trial order completed and dispatched within 24 hours.

---

# 🔥 4. STAGE 3: ACTIVE DEALER PHASE

- **Primary Objective:** Drive immediate inventory rotation on the counter.  
- **Execution SOP:**  
  1. Mobilize CRE to link 5 local painters to the dealer shop via **Swatch Saathi WhatsApp Engine**.  
  2. Install high-impact POSM counter display & co-branded shopfront banner.  
  3. Perform a live trowel application demo on a nearby construction site.  
- **Stage Success Metric:** First voluntary reorder placed within **15–30 days** of trial delivery.

---

# 🚀 5. STAGE 4: GROWTH PARTNER PHASE

- **Primary Objective:** Scale monthly order volume from 50 to 200 bags/month.  
- **Execution SOP:**  
  1. Enroll dealer in the **DGP (Dealer Growth Partner)** quarterly volume rebate scheme.  
  2. Host a localized **Swatch Karigar Painter Meet** funded jointly by Swatch Paints.  
  3. Run localized WhatsApp broadcast campaign showcasing video site testimonials.  
- **Stage Success Metric:** Consistent month-on-month volume growth ($>15\%\text{ MoM}$).

---

# 👑 6. STAGE 5: POWER DEALER PHASE (MARKET LOCK)

- **Primary Objective:** Establish territory dominance and lock out legacy competitors.  
- **Execution SOP:**  
  1. Grant **DSP (Dealer Strategic Partner)** status with exclusive distribution rights (1 dealer per 20km radius).  
  2. Pass-through maximum trade margins (Counter margin $>₹300/\text{bag}$).  
  3. Guarantee priority factory dispatch and direct milk-run logistics support.  
- **Stage Success Metric:** Swatch Paints captures $>50\%$ of total putty/primer volume at counter.

---

# 🚨 7. STAGE 6: RISK DEALER PHASE (INACTIVITY ALERT)

- **Symptoms:** Zero reorder for $>30\text{ days}$, stock stagnation complaints, or competitor poaching.  
- **Execution SOP:**  
  1. TSO conducts emergency counter visit within 24 hours of system alert.  
  2. Perform stock movement audit & check counter visibility.  
  3. Deploy **Chris Voss Negotiation Engine** to surface hidden grievances.  
  4. Transfer 5 bags directly to an active painter's nearby site to jumpstart rotation.

---

# ♻️ 8. STAGE 7: RECOVERY DEALER PHASE

- **Primary Objective:** Reclaim churned accounts and restore operational trust.  
- **Execution SOP:**  
  1. CCO Agent conducts personal relationship audit with dealer owner.  
  2. Offer a risk-free re-entry trial pack featuring upgraded packaging & SPGP token incentives.  
  3. Re-engage local painter network to drive immediate pull-through.

---

# ⚙️ 9. TERRITORY CONTROL & PROTECTION GOVERNANCE

- **Strict Territory Protection Rules:**  
  - Maximum **1 Power Dealer per 20km radius** to prevent internal margin undercut.  
  - No direct factory sales to contractors within an active Power Dealer's assigned zip code.  
- **Continuous Telemetry Monitoring:**  
  - Monthly Order Volume ($₹\text{ Value}$ and Bag Count).  
  - Counter Stock Rotation Speed ($<15\text{ days}$ target inventory turnaround).  
  - Local Painter QR Token Scans linked to dealer account.
