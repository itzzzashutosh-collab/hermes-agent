# 🟡 3. RAW MATERIAL QUALITY & STORAGE CONTROL

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
Input raw material consistency maintain karke batch-to-batch variation zero karna.

---

## ⚙️ RAW MATERIAL INSPECTION CHECKLIST:
- **Silica & Fillers:** Mesh size verification (100-200 mesh for Rustic Texture), zero moisture agglomeration.
- **Acrylic Binders:** Solid content test (50% solid emulsion standard), pH 7.5–8.5 range check.
- **Water Purity:** Clean de-ionized or filtered water (TDS < 300 ppm, pH neutral).
- **Additives & Biocides:** Defoamers, HEC thickeners, and preservatives batch verification.

---

## ❌ CRITICAL PRODUCTION ERRORS TO AVOID:
- Different raw material suppliers mix karna mid-batch.
- Wet/moisture-contaminated silica bags use karna.

---

# 🟢 4. PRE-MIX STAGE (BASE PREPARATION)

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
Homogeneous liquid slurry prepare karna before powder charging.

---

## ⚙️ PRE-MIX SOP:
1. Charge calculated quantity of clean water into the stainless steel mixing vessel.
2. Add wetting agents, biocides, and defoamer additives under low speed stirring (300-500 RPM).
3. Agitate for **10–15 minutes** until completely dissolved.

---

## ⚠️ PRE-MIX RULES:
- ❌ Do NOT run disperser at high RPM during initial liquid addition.
- ✔ Prevent foam generation (Foam traps air and ruins wet density).

---

# 🔵 5. MAIN MIXING STAGE (GRADUAL POWDER CHARGING)

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
Binder and dry filler powders ka uniform wetting and dispersion.

---

## ⚙️ MAIN MIXING SOP:
1. Introduce Acrylic Binder into the pre-mix slurry under medium agitation (800 RPM).
2. **Gradual Powder Charging:** Slowly feed Silica, Calcium Carbonate, and Titanium Dioxide powders into the vortex over a 15-minute window.

---

## ⚠️ CRITICAL RULE:
> **Powder ek saath ek baare me mat daalo. Continuous gradual addition karo to avoid clumping.**

---

# 🟣 6. HIGH-SPEED DISPERSION & GRINDING

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
Agglomerates break breakdown karke smooth texture & maximum opacity derive karna.

---

## ⚙️ EQUIPMENT SPECIFICATION:
- High-Speed Disperser equipped with 7.5 HP motor, Saw-tooth Cowles blade.
- **Speed:** 1400–1800 RPM dispersion.
- **Duration:** **20–30 minutes**.

---

## ⚠️ CRITICAL DISPERSION ISSUE:
- High-shear friction generates intense heat in the batch, raising slurry temperature to **55°C–60°C**.

---

# 🔴 7. TEMPERATURE CONTROL SYSTEM (MANDATORY OPERATIONAL PROTOCOL)

---

## ❌ THE HEAT THREAT:
Batch temperature $> 45^\circ\text{C}$ causes binder coagulation, thickener breakdown, and premature product curing inside the vessel!

---

## ✅ SWATCH 4-POINT COOLING PROTOCOL:

### 🧩 1. EXTERNAL WATER JACKET / SPRAY:
- Continuous cool water circulation around the vessel outer wall during dispersion.

### 🧩 2. INTERVAL MIXING CADENCE:
> **10 Minutes High Speed Dispersion $\longrightarrow$ 5 Minutes Low Speed Cooling Rest**

### 🧩 3. VENTILATION & AMBIENT CONTROL:
- Mixing vessel MUST be placed away from direct sunlight in a well-ventilated factory bay.

### 🧩 4. TARGET DISPATCH TEMPERATURE:
> **Final Batch Temperature MUST drop below $35^\circ\text{C}$ before packing!**

---

# ⚫ 8. STABILIZATION & DEAERATION

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
Trapped micro-air bubbles remove karke stable wet density derive karna.

---

## ⚙️ STABILIZATION SOP:
- Reduce mixer speed to 200 RPM for **10 minutes**.
- Allow slurry to de-aerate naturally before viscosity testing.

---

# 🧪 9. QUALITY CONTROL & TESTING SYSTEM (QC SOP)

---

## 🎯 QC MANDATORY CHECKPOINTS:

| QC Test | Test Method | Acceptance Criteria | Action if Failed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Consistency / Lumps** | Sieve / Gauge Test | Zero lumps, smooth trowel feel | Re-disperse 10 mins with disperser |
| **Viscosity & Slump** | Stormer Viscometer | $110 - 120 \text{ KU}$ (Rustic Texture) | Adjust with HEC thickener / water |
| **Spread & Coverage** | 1 sq.m Sample Wall Test | Uniform finish, zero cracking | Reject if binder separation occurs |
| **Drying Time Test** | Touch Dry Test | Surface dry in $45 - 60 \text{ mins}$ | Reject if drying $> 120 \text{ mins}$ |

---

## ❌ MANDATORY BATCH REJECTION CRITERIA:
- Grit agglomeration or dry lumps.
- Color shade mismatch or pigment floating.
- Severe viscosity drop due to heat breakdown.

---

# 📦 10. PACKING & BATCH MARKING SYSTEM

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
Leak-proof, accurately weighed, factory-sealed bucket & bag packaging.

---

## ⚙️ PACKING SOP:
1. **Tare Weight Check:** Calibrate digital weighing scale before every batch packing.
2. **Net Weight Accuracy:**
   - 25 kg Bag / Bucket (Tolerance: $\pm 50 \text{ grams}$).
   - 20L Emulsion Bucket (Tolerance: $\pm 50 \text{ ml}$).
3. **Sealing:** Airtight inner poly-lining + heavy-duty bucket lid sealing ring.

---
