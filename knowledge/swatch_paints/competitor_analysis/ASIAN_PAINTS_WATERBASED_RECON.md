# 🎁 2. DEALER SCHEMES & APPLICATOR REBATE FRICTION

1. **Low Counter Profitability:**  
   Asian Paints anchors dealer margins at 7–9% on high-volume items (Putty & Tractor), relying on volume targets paid months later.
2. **Complex Point Slabs:**  
   Rebates are locked behind annual volume slabs, penalizing mid-sized regional dealers who miss target bands by small margins.
3. **Zero Instant Cash to Painters:**  
   Master contractors receive token points redeemable only via physical gifts or year-end catalogues, leaving job-site painters without immediate cash incentives.
