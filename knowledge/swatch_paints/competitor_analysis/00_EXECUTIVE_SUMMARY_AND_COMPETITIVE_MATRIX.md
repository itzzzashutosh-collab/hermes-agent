✅ **Margin Attack**: Offer **18–22% dealer margin** on Swatch Rustic vs. Asian Paints’ 12–25% — undercut on price, exceed on margin.

✅ **Distribution Blitz**: Deploy **deposit-free tinting machine rental** with 6-month contract to reduce switching friction.
