# 🧠 1. EXISTENCE CLARITY (WHY WE EXIST)

Swatch Paints exists to solve a single core problem:

👉 **Indian paint market me dealer aur painter dono underpaid hain.**  
👉 **Profit ka majority hissa MNC brands (Asian/Berger) le jaati hain.**  

### 🎯 OUR PURPOSE:
We exist to:
- **Redistribute profit back to dealers & painters**  
- **Break monopoly of high-investment tinting systems**  
- **Create a faster, higher-margin, simpler paint ecosystem**  

### 🔥 POSITIONING TRUTH:
> **Swatch Paints is not competing with brands.**  
> **Swatch Paints is replacing a broken earning system.**
