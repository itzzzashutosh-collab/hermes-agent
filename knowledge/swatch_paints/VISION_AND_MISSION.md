# 🧠 1. EXISTENCE CLARITY (WHY WE EXIST)

Swatch Paints exists to solve a single core industry problem:

👉 **Both paint dealers and applicators/painters are severely underpaid in the traditional Indian paint market.**  
👉 **The vast majority of industry profits are extracted by legacy MNC brands (Asian Paints, Berger, AkzoNobel).**  

### 🎯 OUR PURPOSE:
We exist to:
- **Redistribute trade profits back to dealers & applicators**  
- **Break the monopoly of capital-intensive colorant tinting systems**  
- **Create a faster, higher-margin, simpler paint distribution ecosystem**  

### 🔥 POSITIONING TRUTH:
> **Swatch Paints is not merely competing with legacy paint brands.**  
> **Swatch Paints is replacing a broken trade earning system.**
