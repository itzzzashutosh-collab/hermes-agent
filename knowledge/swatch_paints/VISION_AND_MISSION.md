# 🎯 VISION (10 YEARS)

Swatch Paints will become:

- **India’s fastest growing paint brand in Tier 2/3 markets**  
- **Painter-driven demand leader**  
- **High-margin dealer-first ecosystem**  
- **Alternative to MNC monopoly** *(Asian Paints, Berger, Nerolac)*  

---

# ⚔️ MISSION (DAILY EXECUTION)

- **50+ dealer visits daily** per team  
- **Continuous painter onboarding**  
- **Fast stock movement** *(zero dead inventory)*  
- **Strong dealer profitability focus**
