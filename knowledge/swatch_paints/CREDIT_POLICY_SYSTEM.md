# 🧠 1. CORE CREDIT PHILOSOPHY

---

## ❌ OLD MODEL (गलत तरीका):
- Blanket credit bina security ke dena  
- Delay follow-up aur uncertain cash recovery  

---

## ✅ SWATCH MODEL (सही तरीका):
> **Controlled Credit → Secured PDC Instrument → Predictable Cash Flow**

---

## 🎯 MAIN RULE:
> **Credit sale is NOT a real sale...**  
> **COLLECTED CASH IN BANK is a real sale! Profit tabhi real hai jab paisa account me aaye.**  

---

# ⚙️ 2. CREDIT ARCHITECTURE (SWATCH STANDARD)

```
[Dealer KYC & Trial Verification] ──► [Credit Limit Approval] ──► [PDC Cheque Collection at Dispatch]
       │
       ▼
[30-Day Tracking Cadence] ──► [Day 25 Friendly Reminder] ──► [Day 30 Cheque Deposit / UPI Clear]
```

---

## 🎯 3 CORE CREDIT RULES:
1. **Maximum Credit Period:** Exactly **30 Days** (Zero extension beyond Day 30).
2. **Mandatory Security Instrument:** Post-Dated Cheque (PDC dated for Day 30) or signed Bank NACH mandate.
3. **No Supply on Default:** Zero additional dispatches if previous 30-day bill is unpaid or bounced!

# 🔵 5. POST DATED CHEQUE (PDC) SECURITY SOP

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
100% financial security cover at the exact moment of stock release.

---

## ⚙️ PDC COLLECTION SOP:
1. **At Dispatch Release:** Collecting signed PDC cheque dated for Day 30 is MANDATORY before vehicle gate pass is issued.
2. **Cheque Verification:**
   - Verify Cheque Signature matches bank account name.
   - Verify Amount written matches invoice total.
   - Verify Date is exactly Day 30 from invoice date.
3. **Safe Storage:** PDC cheques locked in factory fireproof safe and logged into Hermes (`/finance`).

---

## 🎯 CRITICAL RULE:
> **Without Signed PDC Cheque $\longrightarrow$ ZERO Credit Dispatch Allowed!**

# 🔴 7. CHEQUE BOUNCE & DEFAULT MANAGEMENT SOP

---

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE:
Immediate legal & operational action against payment default.

---

## ⚙️ CHEQUE BOUNCE ACTION PROTOCOL:

### DAY 31 (CHEQUE BOUNCE NOTICE):
1. Immediate automated dispatch block in Hermes system (`/finance`).
2. Sales Manager visits dealer shop within 12 hours with bounce memo.
3. **Bounce Penalty Fee:** ₹1,000 administrative charge added to outstanding balance.

### DAY 33 (RESOLUTION WINDOW):
- Dealer MUST clear total invoice + penalty via cash/UPI within 48 hours.

### DAY 37 (LEGAL SECTION 138 NOTICE):
- If unpaid after 7 days of bounce $\longrightarrow$ Legal notice under Section 138 of Negotiable Instruments Act issued through company advocate.
- Territory protection revoked permanently; dealer blacklisted across regional network.

---

## 🎯 GOLDEN RULE:
> **Default = Immediate Supply Block + Permanent Territory Revocation!**
