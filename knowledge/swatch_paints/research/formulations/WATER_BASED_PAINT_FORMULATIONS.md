# WATER_BASED_PAINT_FORMULATIONS.md

## Interior Emulsion Paint (Premium Grade)

|| Component | Function | Percentage (%) | Notes |
||---|---|---|---|
|| Acrylic Emulsion (Binder) | Film formation, adhesion | 25–30% | Pure acrylic, 45–50% solids; Tronox or Cristal high-performance grade recommended |
|| Titanium Dioxide (TiO₂) | Opacity, whiteness | 16–18% | Rutile R-grade; optimized for high coverage and contrast ratio |
|| Calcium Carbonate (Filler) | Extender, bulk | 20–25% | Co-hiding extender; paired with TiO₂ for cost efficiency |
