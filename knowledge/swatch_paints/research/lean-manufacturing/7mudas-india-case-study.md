# 7 Mudas Elimination Framework in Indian Paint Manufacturing

## Overview
Taiichi Ohno's 7 Mudas framework is adapted for Indian paint factories to eliminate non-value-adding activities. This document synthesizes real-world observations from regional plants in Rajasthan, Gujarat, and Tamil Nadu.

## The 7 Mudas

1. **Defects**
   - Paint batches rejected due to incorrect viscosity, poor opacity, or color inconsistency.
   - Root cause: Calibration drift in grinders, untrained QC staff.

2. **Over-Production**
   - Excess batches produced beyond current order backlog.
   - Amplifies all other wastes; leads to inventory obsolescence.

3. **Waiting**
   - Operators idle during resin mixing due to delayed pigment arrival.
   - Machine downtime awaiting maintenance.

4. **Transporting**
   - Raw materials moved multiple times between warehouse, weighing station, and reactor.
   - Finished paint transferred across multiple floors without conveyors.

5. **Movement**
   - Operators walking 15–20 meters to retrieve tools, gloves, or sample jars.
   - Poor layout of batching stations.

6. **Inappropriate Processing**
   - Using premium acrylic binder in economy-grade distemper.
   - Over-specifying additives (e.g., nano-silica) in interior emulsions.

7. **Inventory**
   - Raw material stockpiles exceeding 60 days of usage.
   - Finished goods piled in corridors due to lack of racking.
