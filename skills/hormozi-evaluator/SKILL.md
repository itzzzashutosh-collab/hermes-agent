## 10. FAILURE MODES & COUNTERMEASURES

### 10.1 Failure Mode: Giving Away Gross Margin on Commodity Products
- *The Trap:* Reps apply Grand Slam bundles and big discounts to low-margin wall putty or distemper, causing factory losses.
- *Countermeasure:* ERP enforces hard constraint: Grand Slam bundles are only valid when high-margin specialty emulsions make up >65% of invoice value.

### 10.2 Failure Mode: Fake Risk Reversal
- *The Trap:* Offering a buyback guarantee with 15 hidden procedural hurdles (original packaging unbroken, notarized stamp paper, 90-day waiting period).
- *Countermeasure:* Make the guarantee completely frictionless: *"Our van driver checks the seal, picks up the bucket, and your ledger is credited immediately."*
