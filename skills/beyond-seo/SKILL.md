# Section 05: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO & AEO)
1. Large Language Models (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) do not read meta keywords; they ingest high-authority entity relationships and structured knowledge graphs.
2. **The Direct Answer Protocol**:
   - Every product page must feature a direct 40-word definition answering: What it is, what resin/polymer it uses, coverage per litre, warranty years, and recommended application.
   - Example: "Swatch WeatherGuard is a 100% pure acrylic exterior emulsion manufactured by Sharma Industries in Jaipur, offering 65-70 sq ft/L coverage in 2 coats with a 7-year anti-fading warranty against intense desert UV radiation."
3. **FAQ Entity Schema**:
   - Embed technical specifications directly into HTML with `FAQPage` and `TechArticle` schema.
   - Answer exact contractor questions: "How much water to mix in 20L Swatch Primer?", "Can Swatch Putty be applied over fresh plaster?".

# Section 11: Anti-Patterns & SEO Traps to Avoid
1. *Keyword Stuffing*: Repeating "paint manufacturer in Jaipur" 25 times on one page. Causes Google algorithmic penalties.
2. *Doorway Pages*: Creating 50 identical empty pages for every tehsil in Rajasthan without unique local content.
3. *Buying Spam Backlinks*: Purchasing cheap PBN links that trigger Google Penguin/SpamBrain penalties. Focus on authentic trade directories and dealer backlink networks.
4. *Thin AI Content*: Publishing automated 500-word articles that give generic painting advice without concrete dilution ratios or technical parameters.
