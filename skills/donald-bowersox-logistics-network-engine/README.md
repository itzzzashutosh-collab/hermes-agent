# Donald Bowersox Logistics Network Engine

This skill enables Swatch Paints to design, optimize, and govern its logistics network using Donald Bowersox’s foundational principles of supply chain network design, focusing on depot placement, full truckload optimization, and responsive logistics systems for the Indian paint industry.

## Purpose
To enable Swatch Paints to design, optimize, and govern its logistics network using Donald Bowersox’s foundational principles of supply chain network design, focusing on depot placement, full truckload optimization, and responsive logistics systems for the Indian paint industry. This skill ensures cost-efficient, high-service logistics operations across Rajasthan and adjacent markets.

## When to Use
Use this skill when:
- Planning new depot locations in Rajasthan or adjacent states
- Optimizing existing depot networks for cost and speed
- Designing full truckload (FTL) routing strategies for bulk deliveries
- Responding to sudden spikes in demand from key territories
- Conducting logistics network audits or redesigns

## Inputs Required
- Current depot locations and capacities
- Regional demand forecasts (next 6 months)
- Transportation costs (per km, per tonne)
- Lead times for delivery to each zone
- Warehouse lease terms and availability
- Vehicle fleet size and capacity (tonnes per truck)
- Historical shipment volumes by route

## Diagnostic Questions
1. What is the current average delivery lead time from depot to painter?
2. Are we experiencing stockouts in high-demand zones despite adequate warehouse stock?
3. Is our FTL utilization below 85%?
4. Are we paying premium freight rates for partial loads?
5. Are there underserved regions with growing contractor activity?

## Core Frameworks
1. **Network Design Optimization (Bowersox et al.)** – Applies cost minimization and service maximization trade-offs in network configuration.
2. **Full Truckload Routing (FTL)** – Maximizes load efficiency and reduces per-unit transportation cost.
3. **Responsive Logistics Systems** – Enables rapid response to demand surges through pre-positioned inventory and flexible routing.
