1. **Cash in the Bank Beats Invoiced Vanity:** A sale is not complete when the invoice is printed; it is complete when the funds clear the Sharma Industries bank account. Commission is paid on collections, never on uncollected debt.
2. **Process Over Heroics:** Great sales organizations are built on repeatable, documented systems. A disciplined B-player following an airtight beat plan will consistently outperform an undisciplined superstar.
3. **Margin-Weighted Volume:** Volume without gross margin is corporate vanity. Reps are incentivized to sell high-margin architectural coatings (Royal Luxury, WeatherShield PU) rather than dumping low-margin commodity putty.
4. **Absolute Secondary Liquidation Governance:** Channel inventory must rotate. If secondary sales stall, primary billing halts immediately to prevent market choking.
