## Examples
1. **Scenario: Overstocked Warehouses Despite Rising Orders**
   - Symptom: Warehouses full, yet delivery SLA missed
   - Diagnosis: Reinforcing loop – sales team pushes volume → ops overproduces → finance delays payments → ops slows dispatch → delivery SLA breached
   - Intervention: Align sales incentives with delivery performance; implement buffer management

2. **Scenario: Rising Dealer Churn Despite Training Programs**
   - Symptom: High training attendance but increasing churn
   - Diagnosis: Delayed feedback loop – field reports take 14 days to reach HQ → fixes deployed after damage
   - Intervention: Implement biweekly dealer pulse surveys; assign escalation path to CEO

- Ignoring feedback loop delays → interventions fail to land
- Treating symptoms instead of root cause → recurring crises
