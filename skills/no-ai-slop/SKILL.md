# Section 05: Commercial Dealer & Contractor Tone Guidelines
1. **Dealers Care About Three Numbers**:
   - Purchase price per bucket / carton (net landed cost).
   - Scheme & cash discount (CD) structure (e.g., 3% CD on 7-day payment, 1 free bucket on 10).
   - Market demand & counter pull (why customer will buy Swatch over Asian Paints / Berger).
2. **Contractors / Painters Care About Three Practicalities**:
   - Flow and levelling (brush drag aur hath ka zor kitna lagta hai).
   - Coverage (ek litre me kitne square feet nikalta hai).
   - Instant token coupon cashback via QR code (seedha bank account me).
3. **Rule of Specificity**: If a sentence can be used unchanged by a software SaaS app, it is prohibited in Swatch Paints. Every statement must mention litres, microns, square feet, days, or rupees.

# Section 06: Factory SOP & Operational Language Discipline
1. Factory workers and batch makers need unambiguous, imperative operational commands.
2. Replace "Ensure adequate dispersion of pigments" with "Run Cowles disperser at 1450 RPM for 25 minutes until Hegman gauge reads under 25 microns."

4. Never use passive voice in SOPs ("The resin was pumped" -> "Pump 450 kg alkyd resin into kettle 2").
5. Include explicit tolerance windows: "Viscosity must be 85 ± 5 seconds on Ford Cup 4 at 30°C."

# Section 11: Anti-Patterns & Common Traps
1. *The Over-Polishing Trap*: Rewriting a dealer's raw, authentic message into high-brow corporate English. Maintain genuine spoken character.
2. *The Generic Metric Trap*: Replacing one abstraction with another (e.g. replacing "highly efficient" with "optimal productivity" instead of "cuts batch cycle time by 45 minutes").
3. *The Artificial Urgency Trap*: Adding "Hurry! Limited time only!" to industrial B2B offers where dealers already know factory lead times.
4. *The Empathy Robot*: Answering customer complaints with "We deeply apologize for any inconvenience caused and understand your frustration." Replace with: "We dispatched a replacement 20L bucket today via Sharma Transport. Our technical officer will visit your site tomorrow at 11 AM."

# Section 16: Automated De-Slopping Transformation Engine
```python
def deslop_text(raw_text: str) -> str:
    """Strip robotic AI patterns from sales copy and plant SOPs."""
    banned_words = [
        'delve', 'foster', 'leverage', 'utilize', 'streamline', 'robust',
        'cutting-edge', 'paradigm shift', 'game-changer', 'tapestry',
        'realm', 'beacon', 'multifaceted', 'meticulous', 'transformative',
        'elevate', 'embark', 'supercharge', 'harness', 'ever-evolving',
        'testament to', 'synergy', 'pivotal', 'holistic'
    ]
    clean = raw_text
    for word in banned_words:
        clean = clean.replace(f' {word} ', ' ')
        clean = clean.replace(f' {word.capitalize()} ', ' ')
    return clean.strip()
```
