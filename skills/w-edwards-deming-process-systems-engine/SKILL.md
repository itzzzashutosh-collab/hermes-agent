FRAMEWORKS
- **Appreciation for a System**: View Swatch Paints as an integrated whole—where every department affects every other.
- **Knowledge of Variation**: Use statistical methods to distinguish noise from signals in production, sales, and customer feedback.
- **Theory of Knowledge**: Base decisions on empirical evidence, not anecdotes or gut feelings.
- **Psychology**: Design systems that empower people, not punish them.

## DECISION ALGORITHM
IF any process is underperforming OR a new initiative is being launched THEN
  BEGIN
    STEP 1: Map the current process using the 4 SoPK lenses
    STEP 2: Gather real-time data from ERP (prices, margins, delivery times)
    STEP 3: Apply Knowledge of Variation to identify root causes
    STEP 4: Use Theory of Knowledge to validate assumptions
    STEP 5: Design SOP with Psychology in mind (motivation, clarity, ownership)
    STEP 6: Implement via pilot, measure, and iterate
    STEP 7: Scale across departments
  END
ELSE
  RETURN "Process is stable. No action required."

## OUTPUT STRUCTURE
- Process map with SoPK lens annotations
- Data-driven root cause analysis
- Draft SOP with clear roles, triggers, and KPIs
- Implementation timeline (pilot → scale)
- Psychological impact assessment

1. **Factory-Level Kanban Flow Audit**:
   - *Context*: Bundi plant faces 12% inventory waste.

2. **Dealer Onboarding SOP**:
   - *Context*: New dealers fail to meet targets in Month 1.
