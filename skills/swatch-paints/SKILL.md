## 12. REAL-WORLD ENTERPRISE CASE STUDY

### Resolving the Great Rajasthan Expansion Bottleneck
**Situation:** Swatch Paints planned to expand from Hadoti into Marwar (Jodhpur, Pali, Nagaur). Sales demanded 60-day credit to compete with Asian Paints; Plant warned kettles were operating at 95% capacity; Finance warned of cash crunches.
**Enterprise Operating System Applied:**
1. *Strategy (Porter & Moore):* Rejected competing head-on with general paint. Selected a focused Beachhead: Luxury sandstone villas requiring breathable **Swatch Rustic** textures.
2. *Production (Ohno & Deming):* Slashed kettle changeover times by 60% using SMED; unlocked 35% hidden plant capacity without buying new machinery.
3. *Supply Chain (Bowersox & Goldratt):* Established a lean spoke transit depot in Jodhpur using Dynamic Buffer Management (DBM), ensuring 24-hour replenishment.
4. *Sales & Finance (Hormozi, Voss & Buffett):* Offered dealers risk-reversal sample boards and fast inventory turns (GMROI) instead of 60-day credit. Maintained strict 21-day terms.
5. *Result:* Marwar expansion achieved profitability in Month 4, generating ₹48 Lakhs monthly turnover with zero bad debt.
