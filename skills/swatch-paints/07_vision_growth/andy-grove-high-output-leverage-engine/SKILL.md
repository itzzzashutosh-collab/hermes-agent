3. **The 1-on-1 is the Subordinate's Meeting:** Structured weekly 45-minute 1-on-1 meetings are sacred; they are driven by the subordinate's outline to uncover road-blocks and teach operational judgment.
4. **Manage the Limiting Step:** In every business process—whether mixing paint, delivering orders, or collecting cash—identify the single operation that takes the longest and build the entire schedule around it.

## 7. CORE FRAMEWORKS

### 7.1 Grove’s Managerial Leverage Equation
$$\text{Manager's Output} = L_1 \times A_1 + L_2 \times A_2 + L_3 \times A_3 \dots$$
- **High Positive Leverage ($L > 10$):** Training 15 field sales reps on the Straight Line script; designing a 1-page visual SOP for kettle cleanout; conducting a rigorous 1-on-1.
- **Negative Leverage ($L < -10$):** Arriving unprepared to a meeting of 8 people; meddling in a subordinate's autonomous decision; creating vague policies that confuse the field.

### 7.2 The Limiting Step Model (The Breakfast Factory)
```
[RAW CHEMICALS DISPENSED] ──► 15 Minutes
              │
              ▼
[BEAD-MILL GRINDING (THE LIMITING STEP)] ──► 50 MINUTES (GOVERNS ENTIRE SCHEDULE!)
              │
              ▼
[LET-DOWN & TINTING]      ──► 20 Minutes
              │
              ▼
[AUTOMATED PACKAGING]     ──► 15 Minutes
```
- **The Rule:** Build the schedule backwards from the 50-minute Limiting Step. Stagger all upstream and downstream operations so the limiting step NEVER waits for work.

   - Ban finger-pointing; review live ERP production queues and buffer data; agree on root-cause counter-measures within 30 minutes.

2. Track quarterly OKR milestones with transparent weekly red/yellow/green progress updates.

## 12. REAL-WORLD INDIAN PAINT FIELD EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Cutting Order-to-Dispatch Lag by Eliminating the Credit Approval Limiting Step
**Situation:** Dealer orders took an average of 28 hours from order placement to truck dispatch. Sales blamed warehouse loaders, and warehouse loaders blamed plant production.
**Grove Limiting Step Analysis Applied:**
- Mapped the entire order-to-dispatch black box. Discovered that mixing paint took 2 hours and loading took 1 hour, but the order sat idle for 19 hours waiting for the Credit Manager to manually verify bank statements and sign a paper credit slip!
- The manual credit check was the **Limiting Step** strangling the entire supply chain.
- Implemented high-leverage automated ERP credit rules: If a dealer has zero overdue invoices >30 days and order is within credit limit, system approves dispatch instantly in 3 seconds.
- Order-to-dispatch time collapsed from 28 hours to 4 hours, transforming customer satisfaction across all territories.
