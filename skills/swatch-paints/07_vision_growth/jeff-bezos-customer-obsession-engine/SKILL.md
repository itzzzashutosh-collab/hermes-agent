## 10. STEP-BY-STEP TACTICAL EXECUTION PLAYBOOK

### Phase 1: Pre-Flight Working Backwards (PR/FAQ)
1. The project champion writes a 1-page Press Release and 2-page FAQ working backwards from the contractor.
2. Read the memo in complete silence for the first 15 minutes of the review meeting.
3. If the customer value proposition is not overwhelmingly clear: Reject the project immediately before spending capital.

### Phase 2: Live In-Field / Operational Execution Protocol
1. **The Correction of Errors (COE) Protocol:**
   - When a major operational failure occurs (e.g. an entire batch of paint delivered with wrong tint shade):
   - Convene a COE session within 48 hours.
   - Execute the **5 Whys**: Drill down to the systemic root cause (e.g. dispenser software calibration drift).
   - Implement permanent mechanical/software fail-safes; publish the COE across all plants.
2. **Empower Type 2 Decisions:**
   - Territory managers authorized to approve retail promotions up to ₹25,000 on their own signature.

### Phase 3: Post-Execution Follow-Up, ERP Entry & Locking
1. Log all COE systemic fixes and Type 2 experiment outcomes in the ERP Operational Knowledge Vault.
2. Track Customer Effort Score and delivery defect PPM weekly.
3. Review Day 1 Vitality and Customer Obsession metrics during the monthly Executive Session with Ashutosh Sharma Sir.

## 12. REAL-WORLD INDIAN PAINT FIELD EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Working Backwards to Create the 30kg Swatch Rustic Easy-Pour Drum
**Situation:** Traditional architectural textures in India were sold in unwieldy, smooth-sided 30kg plastic drums. Contractors complained that workers constantly dropped them, broke fingernails trying to pry open the heavy lids, and wasted 2 kg of thick texture stuck to the bottom ridges.
**Bezos Working Backwards Process Applied:**
- R&D started by writing a Press Release from the perspective of an Indian painter: *"Today, Swatch Paints introduced the Easy-Pour Ergonomic Texture Drum that cuts wall application fatigue in half."*
- Re-engineered the packaging working backwards from the job site:
  1. Molded dual ergonomic heavy-duty side lifting handles.
  2. Designed a wide-mouth leakproof screw-lid with a quick-release lever (no screwdrivers required).
  3. Engineered smooth interior walls with zero internal ribs, allowing 100% of the texture to slide out clean.
- Contractors went wild for the new drum. Swatch Rustic became the overwhelming favorite of painters across Hadoti, driving a 55% surge in repeat contractor orders.
