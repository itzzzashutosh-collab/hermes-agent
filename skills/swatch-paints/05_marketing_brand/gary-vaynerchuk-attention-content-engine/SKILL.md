3. **Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook:** Give 3 pieces of pure value (painting tips, business advice for thekedars, surface preparation guides) before asking for a single paint sale.
4. **Engage with Every Single Comment:** Social media is a bilateral conversation, not a broadcast loudspeaker; reply to 100% of painter comments and direct messages within 30 minutes.

- Over 220 homeowners and 65 master thekedars sent direct messages asking for dealer locations.
- Resulted in ₹18 Lakhs in verified secondary sales of Swatch Rustic across Jaipur dealers within 3 weeks.
